EcoLife is a mobile application designed to help users adopt a more sustainable and eco-friendly lifestyle. Track your environmental impact, discover green alternatives, and join a community of like-minded individuals working towards a healthier planet.

Features ✨
Carbon Footprint Tracker 📊
Monitor your daily carbon emissions with our easy-to-use calculator.

Sustainable Challenges 🏆
Participate in weekly eco-challenges to reduce waste and conserve resources.

Green Product Directory 🛍️
Discover eco-friendly alternatives for everyday products.

Local Recycling Guide ♻️
Find recycling centers and proper disposal methods in your area.

Community Forum 💬
Connect with other eco-conscious users to share tips and experiences.

Educational Resources 📚
Learn about sustainability through articles, videos, and interactive content.

Getting Started 🚀
Prerequisites
iOS 14+/Android 8.0+

100MB of available storage

Installation
Download from the App Store or Google Play

Create an account or sign in with Google

Complete your profile and set your sustainability goals
